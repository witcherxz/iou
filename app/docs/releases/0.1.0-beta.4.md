# IoU 0.1 Beta 4

This release makes reminders reliable on Android and replaces typed dates with a calendar.

## Reminders

- Reminders now use exact alarms. Earlier builds had no exact-alarm permission, so Android could postpone reminders while the phone slept or when IoU had not been opened for a while — sometimes until after the moment had passed. IoU now declares `USE_EXACT_ALARM` (Android 13+; `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` on Android 12), which is granted at install.
- New **وصول التذكيرات** card on the Reminders screen shows whether reminders are exact and whether IoU is exempt from battery optimisation. **السماح بالعمل في الخلفية** opens Android's prompt to exempt it. Samsung phones also get guidance to keep **دفتر الديون** out of the sleeping and deep-sleeping app lists.
- Android now keeps the next 200 reminders scheduled instead of 60. If even those run out before IoU is opened, a final **افتح الدفتر لمتابعة التذكيرات** notification appears instead of reminders silently stopping.
- Force-stopping IoU (including some Samsung "optimise" actions) still cancels its reminders until the app is opened again; Android does not allow any app to avoid this.

## Date picker

- Every date field now opens an in-app Arabic calendar: Sunday-first weeks, month arrows, tap the month title to jump to a year, then **تم**. Dates outside the allowed range are disabled. It is not the Android system picker, which would trigger the app lock.

## Fixes

- Short labels such as **كل يوم** and **كل أسبوع** could appear as **كل** on Samsung phones. Text now uses simple line breaking to avoid the hidden wrapped word. This was seen on the user's phone and could not be reproduced on the emulator.

Install `iou-0.1.0-beta.4.apk` over Beta 3. Android version code is `14`; the production identity, signing key and backup formats are unchanged. After installing, open **التذكيرات**, tap **السماح بالعمل في الخلفية**, and on Samsung check that **دفتر الديون** is not in the sleeping-app lists.

TypeScript and the integrated checks passed, including new calendar, reminder-queue and release-permission checks; the release verifier now rejects an APK without the exact-alarm permissions or the reminder health module. A debug-signed QA build on the Android 16 emulator confirmed exact alarms, an on-time reminder (86 ms) in forced deep Doze without battery exemption, the battery prompt, a 200-reminder queue with renewal notice, and the calendar picker. The user's Samsung phone has not yet been tested with this build.
