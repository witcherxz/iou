# IoU 0.1 Alpha 7

Debt editing now includes **تغيير يوم الاستحقاق الشهري**. Enter a day from 1 to 31 and choose **تطبيق على الأقساط المتبقية** to preview all remaining installment dates together, then save once. Each installment stays in its current month; shorter months use their last day. Fully paid or forgiven installments stay unchanged. Partly settled installments with a remaining balance move with the rest. This works for money owed to you and money you owe.

Individual date edits remain available. The preview shows previous dates, and Save records one correction with the previous schedule available through Undo. Invalid dates or collisions are rejected. Save changes to installment amounts before using the bulk date control so completed installments are identified from the saved ledger.

Includes the recent fixes for draft preservation when adding a person, OS amount keyboards, automatic biometrics with PIN fallback, and Android backup confirmations that stay inside the app window.
